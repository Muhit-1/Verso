Wallpaper

What plays behind the widgets — an image, a video, or an interactive page.

None set — the desk's own colours show.

Videos, GIFs and interactive pages play behind your desktop icons and taskbar, and pause by themselves while a full-screen app or a maximised window is in front. An image becomes your Windows wallpaper.

Widgets

Choose what shows up on your desktop.

Each widget is independent: switching one off never moves another. To move or resize them, open the round ! button on the desk and choose Arrange. Positions are remembered.

Apps

Your app library. New apps land in Recent; move them into your own boxes to organise them. The same boxes show on the desk while the App launcher widget is on.
